Fixed-grid overlap experiment

Generated by experiments/fixed-grid-overlap/analyze.py. Every number on this page is computed from the files in this folder.

Question

All candidates keep the 436 NYU cells at the NYU size and spacing; only the assignment of districts to cells changes. Which layout properties move together with the morph overlap?

Setup

  • Discovery set: Fixed NYU grid, Noise 0, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.4 × Transform 0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8, 1, seed 42 (30 distinct assignments).
  • Replication set: the same 30 settings with seeds 1–4, Noise > 0 only (96 distinct assignments). It shares no candidate with the discovery set.
  • Within one Noise level: both sets pooled and split by Noise (0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.4; 30 candidates each), correlated inside each level, then averaged. This asks whether a property still goes with overlap when the amount of noise is held fixed.
  • Overlap: the morph page's own 101 samples (index.html, read by evidence/morph_scores.cjs). Layout metrics: evidence/evaluate_layouts.py.

Limits

  • All candidates come from one generator (Hungarian assignment to transformed centroids) on one fixed grid; other generators may behave differently.
  • Overlap is a raster estimate at 101 samples, as on the morph page; it is not a continuous-time measure.
  • The Forsch hybrid is our adaptation (precomputed core keyframes retargeted to each layout), not the full method of Forsch et al.
  • Correlation is not cause: properties that move together with overlap here are candidates to test, not proven drivers.
