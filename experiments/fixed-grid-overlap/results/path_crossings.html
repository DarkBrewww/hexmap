Path-crossing test

Generated by experiments/fixed-grid-overlap/path_crossings.py from the same 126 candidates as report.html.

Hypothesis

Direction error goes with overlap (report.html). The proposed reason: in the Linear morph each district moves on a near-straight line to its cell, so two neighbours that swap sides must cross paths and cover each other on the way. If so, the number of crossing paths should track overlap at least as well as direction error, and should account for direction error's link to overlap.

Measures

  • Crossing pairs (primary, chosen before looking at results): pairs of districts whose straight centre paths, map centroid to cell centre, intersect.
  • Crossing pairs, map neighbours only: the same, for the 1,136 neighbour pairs.
  • Close passes: pairs whose centres come within 0.25, 0.5 or 1 hex step of each other part-way through the morph. Every district moves at the same pace, so this uses timing as well as geometry.
  • Mean path length: how far centres travel, in hex steps (one hex step = 24.10 screen units).

Limits

  • Straight centre paths describe the Linear morph; the Forsch hybrid moves outlines differently, so for it these are rougher proxies.
  • A crossing only counts pairs whose paths meet somewhere; two districts can cross the same spot at different moments.
  • Centres stand in for whole outlines: large districts can overlap without their centres coming close.
  • Same candidates as report.html: one generator on one fixed grid.
